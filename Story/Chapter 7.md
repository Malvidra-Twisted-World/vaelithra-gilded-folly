## Vaelithra’s Intervention (Arena Pick-up)

To help players manage the restrictive **Dominion Zones**, the following appears in **Round 2**:

### Temporal Anchor Points
*Fixed Positions*

> “Three glowing runes appear on the floor, vibrating with a frequency that counteracts the hotel's pressure.”

**Interaction:** Standing on a rune (No action required).
**Effect:** While standing on a rune, a player is **immune to the speed reduction and disadvantage** of the Dominion Zones.
**Mechanical Trigger:** This makes it much easier for players to reach the requirement of "Ending their turn outside zones" or reaching Auric Vex to force him to move, which triggers **Breaking Control**.

## Core Mechanic: Dominion Field

Auric Vex exerts control over the battlefield.

---

### Dominion Zones

At the start of his turn:

- Create **2 control zones** (10 ft radius)

Zones last until next turn.

---

### Effects Inside Zones

Creatures inside:

- Speed halved  
- Disadvantage on attack rolls  
- Cannot take reactions  

---

### Control Expansion

If both players end their turn inside zones:

- Auric gains:
  - +2 AC  
  - Advantage on attacks  

---

## Weakness: Breaking Control

If both players:

- End their turns **outside all zones**
- OR force Auric to move

Then:

- Dominion Field collapses temporarily  
- Auric loses control bonuses  
- Takes +2d6 damage on first hit
