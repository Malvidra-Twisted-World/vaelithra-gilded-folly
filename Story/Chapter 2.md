# Vaelithra: Gilded Folly
## Chapter 2: The Assigned Rooms

---

## Chapter Overview

This chapter focuses on:

- Exploration of the hotel interior  
- Interaction with Vanka Yuriv  
- Introduction to other inmates  
- Subtle foreshadowing of Vaelithra  
- Psychological setup before Trial I  

No combat occurs in this chapter.

---

After the argument between Mammeth and Alethia:

The tension fades.

## Room Assignment

Vanka produces keys.

Each key is:

- Gold-plated  
- Warm to the touch  
- Engraved with the character’s name (even if never spoken)

---

### Vanka Dialogue

“Please… follow the hallway to your right.”

“Everything you need will be inside…”

(Softer)

“...Everything you want, too.”

---

## Vanka’s Room Service (Trial I Prep)

Before the players leave for their rooms, Vanka pulls the chosen champion aside (or leaves a package).

### Item: The Spendthrift's Coin

*Consumable*

> “A heavy gold coin that feels unnervingly warm.”

**Effect:** Use a **Bonus Action** to throw this coin at a target. It explodes into a shower of sparks.
**Mechanical Trigger:** This automatically triggers **Cracks in the Hoard** on Vauryx.

---

## Area 2: The Hallway

### Description

- Long corridor with red carpet  
- Golden wall panels  
- Doors evenly spaced  
- No visible end  

### Atmosphere

- Sound feels muffled  
- Footsteps echo slightly delayed  
- The hallway feels longer than it should be  

---

## Entering the Rooms

Each player finds their room unlocked upon approach.

---

## Area 3: Personalized Rooms

Each room is:

- Luxurious  
- Perfectly tailored  
- Deeply personal  
---

### Core Rule

> The room gives the character what they *desire*  
> But withholds what they *need*

---

## Examples (DM Guidance)

Use player backstory.

### If the character desires wealth:
- Room filled with gold  
- But no exit, no purpose  

### If the character desires comfort:
- Perfect bed, food, warmth  
- But silence is oppressive  

### If the character desires connection:
- Illusions of people  
- They never respond properly  

---

## Subtle Punishment

Players should feel:

- Something is missing  
- Something is wrong  
- The comfort is *empty*

---

## Optional Scene: Other Inmates

Players may leave their rooms.

If they do, introduce:

### Inmate Types

- A noble dining alone with untouched food  
- A warrior polishing a weapon endlessly  
- A merchant counting coins repeatedly  

---

### Key Theme

> Everyone here is trapped in their own version of “enough”

---

## NPC Interaction: Vanka (Follow-up)

If approached again:

She is more nervous.

---

### Dialogue

“P-Please don’t stay outside too long…”

“They’ll notice…”

If asked *who*:

She freezes.

“…management.”

---

## Foreshadowing: Vaelithra

Introduce subtle anomalies:

- A clock ticks… then skips backward  
- A reflection blinks before the player does  
- A door briefly shows a different room  

---
