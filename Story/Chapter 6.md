---

### Sound Delay

- Voices overlap slightly

## Vanka’s Room Service (Trial III Prep)

Vanka is seen cleaning a small silver bell. She "accidentally" leaves it behind for the players.

### Item: Bellhop’s Service Bell
*Consumable*

> “A simple silver bell. When it rings, the sound doesn't echo—it cuts through other sounds.”

**Effect:** Use a **Reaction** when an enemy creates a zone or moves.
**Mechanical Trigger:**
- **Vs Auric Vex:** Instantly collapses one **Dominion Zone**, or allows a player to move 10ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks to escape a zone.
