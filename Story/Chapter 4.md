## The Observer’s Insight (Spectator Mechanic)

While one or two players are in the arena, the others watch via a *Major Image* in the lobby. To keep them engaged:

### 1. Tactical Analysis
**Mekanik:** A spectator can use their Action to make a **DC 15 Insight or Investigation Check**.
- **Success:** The DM reveals one Trait or Resistance of the current Boss. 
- *"You notice the way Vauryx flinches whenever you spend a resource... his gold seems brittle there."*

### 2. Emotional Support / Distraction
**Mekanik:** Once per round, a spectator can "shout" through the image. 
- **Effect:** The Champion gets **Advantage** on their next attack roll, OR the Boss gets **Disadvantage** on their next attack (representing the spectator spotting a gap).

### 3. Resource Transfer (Greed/Charity)
**Mekanik:** A spectator can "throw" a Health Potion or a piece of equipment into the image.
- **Greed Path:** Mammeth takes a "cut" (the potion only heals for half).
- **Charity Path:** Alethia allows it, but the spectator takes 1d10 damage from the planar friction.

## Vanka’s Room Service (Trial II Prep)

Before the next trial begins, Vanka is seen nervously polishing a tray. She leaves it on a nearby table as she "rushes" away.

### Item: Mirror-Polished Serving Tray
*Consumable*

> “A silver tray polished to a terrifying clarity. You see things in it that aren't there—or perhaps things that shouldn't be.”

**Effect:** Use an **Action** to reflect light or "reality" at one target.
**Mechanical Trigger:**
- **Vs Feltheris:** Forces him to move 5ft (breaking **Perfect Alignment**).
- **Vs Citrina:** She becomes fascinated by her own reflection, counting as "Ignored" for 1 round (triggering **Gaze of Value** penalties).
