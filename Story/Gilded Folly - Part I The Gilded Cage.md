# Part I: The Gilded Cage

> *"Welcome to Gilded Folly Jail Hotel..."*

Before the story begins, ask each player what their character was accused of, whether they're actually guilty, and what — if anything — they regret. All characters start at **Level 1** and begin the adventure as prisoners.

## Arrival

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> Cold iron bites into your wrists. The air smells of polished marble — clean, expensive... unnatural. You are escorted forward in silence. Massive golden doors open. And then — you are no longer in a prison. A grand lobby stretches before you: marble floors reflecting chandelier light, velvet furniture untouched, a silence that feels *intentional*. Behind a polished desk stands a woman adjusting her glasses nervously. "W-Welcome to Gilded Folly Jail Hotel..."

## The Lobby

The lobby is comfortable but unsettling — marble floors inlaid with gold, chandeliers casting warm, unnatural light, no guards anywhere in sight, and sound that echoes just slightly too long. It feels too clean, too perfect, as though it's watching.

## Vanka Yuriv

Behind the desk stands **Vanka Yuriv** — a young woman, bespectacled, nervous in posture, who avoids eye contact and speaks softly and quickly, adjusting her glasses whenever she's uncertain.

> "Y-Your room has already been prepared…"
>
> "Please… try not to wander too far…"

***DM Notes.*** Vanka is a Human Vaelithra — though nothing in this chapter should confirm that outright. She knows more than she lets on, and occasionally reacts to something before it happens. Let this be a quiet, background wrongness rather than a spotlighted mystery.

## The Argument

Shortly after arrival, or after the party interacts with Vanka, this event triggers.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> The air grows heavy. A ripple moves through the marble floor. Two figures appear where there was nothing before. One radiates a calm, unwavering presence. The other gleams with gold, smiling as if amused by everything. "You call this justice?" the radiant one says. "I call it efficiency," the golden one replies.

***The Active Observers.*** **Avariel Mammeth**, Demon of Greed, and **Alethia Caelion**, Angel of Charity, aren't just watching — they're competing for the souls of the party. Mammeth claims he punishes criminals, that society benefits, that profit is justified. Alethia argues this is exploitation, that suffering is being used rather than resolved. Full write-ups for both, including their once-per-session player-facing offers (the Greed Bargain and the Charity Sacrifice), are in *Running the Adventure*.

Once they notice the party, Mammeth murmurs, *"Ah… fresh assets,"* while Alethia says simply, *"Champions."*

***Transition to Trials.*** Mammeth gestures lazily: *"Let's make this interesting."* Alethia responds: *"Then let it be fair."* The world shifts. The lobby distorts. Something deeper awakens. Every player feels it — a quiet question: *what are you willing to give?*

***Running This Scene.*** Don't rush into combat. Let the players talk to Vanka, react to the two entities, and explore the lobby briefly before moving on. This isn't a normal prison — everything here is too perfect, and the danger, for now, stays subtle.

***Foreshadowing.*** Sprinkle in small, deniable anomalies as the party lingers: reflections that lag half a second behind, sounds that echo incorrectly, Vanka reacting to something just before it happens, a moment where time itself feels "off."

## The Assigned Rooms

After the confrontation fades — too quickly, too cleanly, as if nothing had happened — Vanka forces a small smile. *"Y-your rooms are ready…"* She produces keys: gold-plated, warm to the touch, each engraved with a character's name even if it was never spoken aloud.

> "Please… follow the hallway to your right."
>
> "Everything you need will be inside…"
>
> *(softer)* "...Everything you want, too."

***Vanka's Room Service.*** Before the party splits up, Vanka pulls the character most likely to be chosen for Trial I aside, or simply leaves a package behind — a heavy gold coin that feels unnervingly warm. This is **the Spendthrift's Coin**; see *Appendix A: Magic Items*.

## The Hallway

A long corridor, red carpet underfoot, golden wall panels, doors spaced evenly with no visible end. Sound feels muffled here; footsteps echo with a faint, unnatural delay; the hallway feels longer than it should. Each player's room stands unlocked the moment they approach it.

## Personalized Rooms

Every room is luxurious, perfectly tailored, and deeply personal — built around one simple, cruel rule: **the room gives the character what they desire, but withholds what they need.**

***Running the Rooms.*** Build each room from that character's backstory. A character who desires wealth gets a room filled with gold, but no exit and no purpose. A character who desires comfort gets a perfect bed, food, and warmth, but oppressive silence. A character who desires connection gets illusions of people who never quite respond right. The goal is for players to feel that something is missing, something is wrong, that the comfort is *empty* — not through exposition, but through the shape of the room itself.

## Other Inmates

If the party leaves their rooms, they encounter a handful of fellow prisoners, each trapped in a different form of Greed. Introduce two or three:

***The Endless Noble*** sits at a table full of food, eating slowly and carefully, never finishing anything. *"I must preserve the experience,"* he says. He will never feel satisfied.

***The Counting Merchant*** sits surrounded by coins, counting, recounting, recounting again. *"If I lose count… I lose everything."* He grows distressed if interrupted.

***The Perfect Warrior*** polishes a flawless blade, again and again. *"It is not ready yet."* It never will be.

Every inmate here has *enough* — and cannot move forward.

***Vanka, Revisited.*** If approached again, Vanka is more nervous than before. *"P-please don't stay outside too long… They'll notice…"* If asked who, she freezes: *"…management."*

***Night Phase (Optional).*** If the party rests, sleep comes easily — too easily, and their dreams feel structured, organized, observed. One player may also receive a brief visit from **Alethia**: *"You are not here by accident. You are being measured."*

## Ending the Chapter

After sufficient exploration, time subtly accelerates and the hotel grows quieter. Each player feels a pull, a pressure, a selection.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> Somewhere in the distance — a bell rings. Not loud. But undeniable. Once.
