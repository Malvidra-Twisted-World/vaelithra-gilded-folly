# Vaelithra: Gilded Folly
## Chapter 8: Between Trials — The Awakening Structure

---

## Chapter Overview

This chapter represents a turning point.

- The hotel (Vaelithra) begins actively awakening  
- Time anomalies become undeniable  
- Vanka partially breaks her disguise  
- Greed vs Charity influence becomes visible  
- Mammeth begins losing control  

No forced combat.

## Vanka’s Room Service (Trial IV Prep)

Vanka hands a small, unlit candle to the players. Her hands are shaking.

### Item: Aromatic "Silence" Candle
*Consumable*

> “The wax is grey and smells of nothing at all. It feels like a void in your palm.”

**Effect:** Use a **Bonus Action** to light. The candle burns for 1 round.
**Mechanical Trigger:**
- **Vs Aurelion:** All **Accumulation Stacks** reset to 0.
- **Vs Seraphel:** Breaks the **Shared Burden** link for 1 round.
