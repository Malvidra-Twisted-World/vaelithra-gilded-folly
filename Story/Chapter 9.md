## Vaelithra’s Intervention (Arena Pick-ups)

To help manage the complex interaction of Stacks and Shared Damage, two shards appear in **Round 3**:

### 1. The Shard of Stillness
*Consumable (Free Action)*

> “A piece of the floor that has stopped reflecting anything. It is perfectly calm.”

**Effect:** Crushing this shard resets all of **Aurelion’s Accumulation Stacks** to 0 immediately.
**Mechanical Trigger:** Use this if the DM or players find the stack management becoming too overwhelming.

### 2. The Prism of Isolation
*Object (Area Effect)*

> “A floating glass shard that creates a 'dead zone' in reality around it.”

**Effect:** While within 5ft of this shard, damage cannot be transferred.
**Mechanical Trigger:** If **Seraphel** is forced or lured near this shard, his **Shared Burden** trait is disabled, making him vulnerable to the **Isolation** weakness.
