## Chapter 1: The Gilded Folly Jail Hotel

---

## Adventure Overview

*Gilded Folly* is a narrative-driven one-shot where the characters are prisoners inside a luxurious jail disguised as a hotel.

- Characters begin as inmates (guilty or not is optional)
- They are forced to participate as **champions**
- Two entities oppose each other:
  - **Greed** (Avariel Mammeth)
  - **Charity** (Alethia Caelion)
- The hotel itself is a **Vaelithra (Fragment of Vaeil)**

---

## Adventure Background

After the **Sundering War**, the God of Time, **Vaeil**, altered the timeline to end the war.

To protect the **Archive of Time**, he shattered himself into 12 fragments known as **Vaelithra**.

These fragments became:
- People
- Objects
- Creatures
- Places

One such fragment became:

> **The Gilded Folly Jail Hotel**

A prison where:
- Every inmate is given luxury
- Their punishment is tied to their sin

The hotel was unknowingly purchased by:

- **Avariel Mammeth**, Demon of Greed

He now uses it as:
- A place of punishment
- A stage for divine trials
- A source of profit

## Entities: The Active Observers

Mammeth and Alethia are not just watching; they are competing for the souls of the champions.

### Avariel Mammeth (Demon of Greed)
**Roleplay:** He treats the trial like a high-stakes gambling match. He wants the players to survive, but only if they "owe" him.

**The Greed Bargain (Once per Session):** 
Mammeth can offer a player a "Loan of Power."
- **Benefit:** Instant Critical Hit or Maximum Damage on one attack/spell.
- **Cost:** After the session, the player must give up one of their most valuable items or suffer a permanent -1 to a stat of Mammeth’s choice (representing interest).

---

### Alethia Caelion (Angel of Charity)
**Roleplay:** She is a silent supporter of growth. She believes in the strength of the group, not the individual.

**The Charity Sacrifice (Once per Session):**
Alethia can offer a "Shared Burden."
- **Benefit:** A player can choose to automatically succeed on a Saving Throw.
- **Cost:** Two other players of the target's choice take 4d10 Radiant damage (it cannot be reduced). Charity requires someone else to carry the weight.
