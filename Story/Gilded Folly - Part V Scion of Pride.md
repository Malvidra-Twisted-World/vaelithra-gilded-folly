# Part V: Scion of Pride

> *"A glitch that bites back. Quaint."*

The party stands at Level 15, fully healed, chosen by a hotel that has finally stopped hiding what it is. Across from them: an entity that came here uninvited, certain nothing in this building can touch him.

## Final Battle: Cael Verix, Scion of Xy'loth

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> "Show me what you believe is strength."

***Running the Combat.*** The party fights **Cael Verix, Scion of Xy'loth** directly (full stats in *Appendix B: Creatures*). This is the adventure's true final boss — a three-phase fight built entirely around punishing repetition and rewarding variety.

***Phase 1 — Observation.*** Cael opens with Adaptive Dominance active: any creature that uses the same action type (attack, spell, ability) on consecutive turns causes him to gain resistance to that damage type, while that creature suffers a stacking -3 penalty to attack rolls that only resets when they vary their approach. His Absolute Standard trait reduces any single hit dealing less than 35 damage to 0 outright — small, chip damage simply doesn't register. His Phase-Lock Divinity prevents him from being dropped below 600 HP in this phase in a single turn; if a single turn's damage would cross that threshold, the excess is ignored and he transitions immediately. Play this phase as pure, confident superiority — nothing the party does yet actually worries him.

***Phase Transition (at 600 HP).*** *"Predictable."* Cael gains **Flawless Counter**: once per round, when he's hit by an attack, he halves the damage and teleports 20 feet. He also gains **Punish the Strong**, a heavy single-target strike aimed squarely at whichever player has dealt the most damage so far, leaving them with disadvantage on their next turn. Phase-Lock Divinity now holds him above 300 HP per turn instead of 600.

***Phase Transition (at 300 HP).*** *"You… adapt?"* Cael loses Adaptive Dominance entirely, but gains **Cracking Superiority**: if he's hit by three different damage sources in a single round, he becomes vulnerable to damage. This is the phase where varied, coordinated offense finally starts to matter more than raw numbers. He also gains **Collapse Reality**, a party-wide DEX save for heavy force damage.

***Support Elements.*** **Fradonic Ra** can use *Solar Flare* once during the fight as a reaction — when a player damages Cael Verix, Fradonic strips all of Cael's damage resistances for the following round. **Lunaris Thoht** can use *Lunar Veil* once as a reaction to Dismissive Pulse or Collapse Reality, granting the whole party immunity to force damage until the end of the current turn. **Mammeth**, watching with fury, may throw a Gilded Shard dealing 50 force damage to Cael the moment he reaches Phase 3 — his fury at the intrusion outweighing his dislike of the party. **Alethia**, judging the party's coordination, grants the whole party 100 temporary HP if they manage to trigger Cracking Superiority.

***Defeat.*** Cael Verix falls at 0 HP.

## The Retreat of Pride

When Cael Verix reaches 0 HP, he doesn't look defeated — only bored, as though his effort has been interrupted by a hotel that woke up at an inconvenient time.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> "A glitch that bites back. Quaint." He steps back into a tear in reality that closes slowly behind him. The hotel begins to shake violently, its marble peeling away to reveal a blinding silver light underneath.

## Vanka's Revelation

Vanka runs into the middle of the arena, tripping over her own feet, her glasses cracked, hands shaking as she keeps trying to straighten them.

> "I-I'm sorry! This is completely outside procedure! That portal... it's a tear caused by a collision of power from the future! Those two were thrown in here because the force of that entity's attack was strong enough to punch straight through the layers of time. And that entity... he just walked in, following his victims!"

***Easter Egg: Visions Through the Rift.*** As the closing portal distorts further, the party catches an impossible glimpse of something: themselves, in the future — older, perhaps, carrying wounds that don't exist yet, or wearing unfamiliar, legendary gear. For some, the portal shows the face of a loved one or relative who has no business being there, smiling with pride as if they'd just watched a great destiny get written. Let each player describe their own glimpse — this is a moment for player agency, not DM narration.

Vanka watches the building around her begin to dissolve into silver light-dust, growing paler by the second.

> "This hotel was actually a secret hiding place from the past. But because their clash of power accidentally created a 'door,' our coordinates leaked! Gilded Folly is terrified — it has to dissolve its own reality and move to another point in time right now, before that horrifying Pride energy can trace what's left of us!"

## The Observers' Realization

Mammeth and Alethia emerge from the remaining shadows, looking at Vanka with a new intensity.

**Mammeth**, laughing flatly, spinning a large gold coin between fingers that now shake slightly: *"An unlisted asset... I bought this place as a property investment, and it turns out I owned the heart of the Keeper of Time herself. No wonder the maintenance costs never made sense."* He looks at Vanka, his cynical smile fading into something more calculating. *"And you, Vanka... I always wondered how an ordinary 'receptionist' could withstand the pressure of my aura without flinching. Turns out you were never just staff. You are this prison's guardian."*

**Alethia** nods slowly, her wings of light dimming as though paying respect to something far older than herself. *"My suspicion is confirmed. The balance in this hotel was always too stable to have come from our argument alone. Vaelithra never leaves a fragment unwatched. You are the thread holding this fabric together, aren't you?"*

Vanka can only straighten her cracked glasses awkwardly, avoiding eye contact. *"I-I was just doing my job! Besides, the pay here wasn't bad... b-but I think my contract just burned down with the building."*

## The Gilded Chronos-Bell

Vanka fumbles through her pockets in a panic and pulls out several small silver bells, each one trembling faintly. She hands one to each hero, her hands still shaking.

> "T-this! Take this! Everyone needs to hold one! This is what's left of this hotel's 'consciousness' before it disappears completely. Please look after them... I have a feeling we'll meet again, though I really hope not like this!"

Each party member receives **the Gilded Chronos-Bell**; see *Appendix A: Magic Items*.

## Conclusion: Sudden Departure

Without warning, Vanka is pulled away by a swirl of silver light. Gilded Folly doesn't collapse into rubble — it dissolves into particles of time.

The party is thrown back to their own point of origin. Their levels return to what they were before the climb to 15. In one player's hand, the Gilded Chronos-Bell still feels faintly cold — the only remaining proof that the luxurious prison ever existed at all. The last thing anyone hears is Vanka's nervous voice, trailing off: *"A-did I turn off the stove in the lobby? Ah... never mind."*

# End of Adventure
