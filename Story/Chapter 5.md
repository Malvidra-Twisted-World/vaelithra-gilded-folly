## Vaelithra’s Intervention (Arena Pick-ups)

To help manage the duo's positioning mechanics, two objects appear in the arena during **Round 2**:

### 1. Gravity Anchor Shards
*Throwable (Bonus Action)*

> “Heavy, dark stones that seem to pull at the air around them.”

**Effect:** Throw at a point within 30ft.
**Mechanical Trigger:** Creates a 5ft pull/push effect. Use this to force **Feltheris** to move, instantly breaking his **Perfect Alignment** trait.

### 2. The Distraction Prism
*Object (Holdable)*

> “A crystal that refracts light into a thousand confusing patterns.”

**Effect:** While a player holds this, **Citrina** must make a DC 14 Wisdom Save or be forced to target the holder.
**Mechanical Trigger:** Allows other players to "Ignore" Citrina for a full round, triggering her **Gaze of Value** penalties (Disadvantage/AC loss).

## Combat Dynamic (Important)

- Feltheris = **control & stability**
- Citrina = **attention & distraction**

Together:

> They punish hesitation and split focus

---

## Running the Fight

---

### Phase 1 — Pressure

- Both attack normally  
- Player feels overwhelmed  

---

### Phase 2 — Pattern

- Player notices:
  - Feltheris weak when moved  
  - Citrina weak when ignored  

---

### Phase 3 — Adaptation

- Player manipulates:
  - Position  
  - Target priority  
  - Movement
