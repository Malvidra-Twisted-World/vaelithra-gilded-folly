# Part II: The First Trials

> *"You will stand."*

The bell has rung once. Somewhere in the hotel, something has already decided who answers it.

## Trial I: The Hoarder of Still Gold

Only one player takes part in this Trial — the rest of the party observes (see *The Observer's Insight* in *Running the Adventure* for how to keep them engaged). Roll randomly, or let the hotel's pull decide, which player is chosen; frame the selection as inevitable, not arbitrary.

***Level Adjustment.*** The chosen player becomes **Level 5** for the duration of this Trial.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> Something shifts within you. Not growth — adjustment. As if this place reshapes you into something... more complete. A bell rings. Once. The world dulls. One of you feels it. A pull. A certainty. You are being chosen. Everything slows. Time stretches — then leaves everything behind. Except you. "You will stand." The floor fractures. Gold seeps upward through the cracks. The world folds inward. Silence.

***The Gilded Chamber.*** The arena is a marble floor veined with gold, coins embedded across the ground, an endless dim space beyond with no visible exit. The air feels still, heavy, watching.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> At the center stands a figure. Tall. Unmoving. His body is layered in gold — not worn. Grown. Coins orbit him slowly. His eyes burn like molten metal. "You will learn," he says, "the value of not wasting what is yours."

***Running the Combat.*** The party faces **Vauryx, Hoarder of Still Gold** (full stats in *Appendix B: Creatures*) alone. Vauryx begins combat in **Aurum Stasis** — resistant to all damage and gaining temporary HP each turn — which only breaks when the player spends a limited resource, takes a real risk, or commits to close combat. If a full round passes without triggering a break, Stasis reasserts itself and Vauryx gains even more temporary HP. During Round 2, **the Unstable Core** appears in the arena — a pulsing sphere of raw time energy that, when touched or kicked, counts as a risky commitment action and breaks Vauryx's Stasis for free.

Run the fight in three unspoken phases: first Vauryx is a wall — slow, controlled, confident, absorbing everything thrown at him; the moment his Stasis first cracks, let the change be visible and let it last; by the third phase he turns aggressive and risk-taking himself, pushing hard for hits. Let your narration carry the mechanic instead of naming it: when the player hesitates, *"the gold tightens"*; when they commit, *"a fracture spreads"*; when they succeed, *"he pauses… uncertain."*

***Defeat.*** Vauryx falls at 0 HP.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> The gold collapses inward. Vauryx falls to one knee. "To lose... is to have risked something." His body crumbles.

The arena dissolves and the party returns to the lobby — unchanged, except that Mammeth and Alethia are watching. Mammeth, almost delighted: *"How refreshing. You chose to spend."* Alethia, quieter: *"You have learned the first truth."*

## Between Trials: The Watching Hotel

No combat is required in this section. The lobby stands exactly as it was — untouched, unchanged, as if nothing had happened. But something lingers: a weight, a gaze. The hotel remembers.

***Level Synchronization.*** All players are now effectively **Level 8** for the next Trial.

The party is free to return to their rooms, explore the hallway further, or speak with other inmates. The hallway itself has begun to change subtly — lighting flickers, doors seem faintly misaligned, sounds echo incorrectly. A door passed earlier may now show a different number than before.

***Inmate Encounters.*** Introduce two or three more inmates, each a different face of Greed — the same **Endless Noble**, **Counting Merchant**, and **Perfect Warrior** from *Part I* work well here if the party hasn't met them yet.

***Vanka, Second Interaction.*** Vanka appears again, more unstable, more aware. *"You… you survived…"* she whispers. *"That means it's working…"* If pressed further, she hesitates, then admits: *"Not everything here belongs to him…"*

***Vanka's Room Service.*** Before the next Trial, Vanka is seen nervously polishing a silver tray, then leaves it behind as she rushes off. This is **the Mirror-Polished Serving Tray**; see *Appendix A: Magic Items*.

***Foreshadowing.*** Escalate the anomalies slightly: a clock ticks forward, then skips backward; a reflection moves independently for a split second; a hallway briefly shows a different layout. Optionally, let one player experience a brief, unexplained memory-glitch — somewhere else, some other time, then back, with no one else reacting.

***Tracking Greed vs. Charity.*** This is a good chapter to start applying the silent system described in *Running the Adventure* — hoarding items, ignoring an inmate's suffering, and choosing self-benefit over the group all nudge the scale toward Greed; helping an inmate, sharing resources, and taking on risk for someone else nudge it toward Charity. Let the environment answer quietly: gold surfaces sharpen and the air grows heavier under Greed, light softens and sound feels more natural under Charity. Don't explain any of this to the players.

Alethia may appear briefly to one player: *"You are not being tested for strength. You are being measured."* Mammeth's voice may echo more casually: *"Interesting choices… Everything has value here."*

***Ending the Interlude.*** When the party is ready to move on:

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> The bell rings again. This time — twice.

## Trial II: The Cost of Reliance

One player is chosen again, and this time faces two champions at once.

***Level Adjustment.*** The chosen player becomes **Level 8**.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> The weight returns. Heavier. Not a pull — an expectation. "You will stand."

***The Divided Vault.*** A split battlefield with uneven terrain, gold chains hanging between pillars, vault doors embedded in the walls, and two opposing elevated positions.

***Running the Combat.*** The party faces **Feltheris, the Golden Warden** and **Citrina, the Lustrous Siren** together (full stats in *Appendix B: Creatures*). The two bosses work as a pair: Feltheris rewards control and stability, gaining +2 AC and advantage on saves whenever he stands still, and losing both the instant he's displaced or forced to move. Citrina rewards attention and distraction, gaining advantage and +2 AC while she's the primary target of attacks, and losing both if she's ignored for a full round. Together, they punish hesitation and split focus. During Round 2, two arena objects appear to help: **the Gravity Anchor Shards**, throwable stones that create a short pull/push effect strong enough to force Feltheris out of position, and **the Distraction Prism**, a crystal a player can hold to force Citrina's attention for a full round, letting the rest of the party safely ignore her.

Run this fight in three phases: first, pure pressure, as both bosses attack normally and the lone player feels overwhelmed; then recognition, as the pattern becomes clear — Feltheris weak when moved, Citrina weak when ignored; then adaptation, as the player starts manipulating position, target priority, and movement to their advantage. Narrate the shift directly: *"he stands perfectly aligned"* versus *"his structure falters"* for Feltheris, *"she smiles — pleased"* versus *"her composure slips"* for Citrina.

***Defeat.*** Both champions must be reduced to 0 HP.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=info,collapse=--> Read Aloud
> The Warden collapses first. Rigid. Silent. The Siren falters next. Her smile fades. "You… refused to choose… …how inefficient…" Both fall.

## Ending the Chapter

The party has learned that risk breaks Greed's grip, and that hesitation and split focus are exactly what it feeds on. Two Trials remain, and the hotel is no longer entirely sure it can contain what it's teaching them. Continue to *Part III: The Weight of Ownership*.
