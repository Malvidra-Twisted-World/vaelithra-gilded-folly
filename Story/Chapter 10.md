# Vaelithra: Gilded Folly
## Final Event — The Intrusion of Pride

---

## Chapter Overview

- The trial system is interrupted  
- A future conflict bleeds into the present  
- Pride enters uninvited  
- Players become the final line of resistance  

---

## Trigger

Immediately after Trial IV concludes.

---

## Mammeth’s Declaration

> Read:

The arena stabilizes.

Perfect.

For the first time.

Mammeth steps forward.

No smile.

No performance.

“It is done.”

“Greed prevails.”

---

## The Rupture

> Read:

The sky above tears.

Not clean.

Not controlled.

Something forces its way through.

The gold around you trembles—

Not in obedience.

In resistance.

---

## The Fall

> Read:

Two figures are thrown from the tear.

Not descending—

Rejected.

They crash into the arena.

Hard.

Power ripples from them—

But unstable.

---

## Fradonic Ra

- Jade green haori  
- Radiates intense **sun energy**  
- Body strained, breathing heavy  

---

## Lunaris Thoht

- Luxurious red attire  
- Emits controlled **moon energy**  
- Eyes sharp, but weakened  

---

## Condition

Both are at:

- **25% HP**
- Unable to fight further (for now)

---

### Dialogue (Optional)

Fradonic:
“…wrong place…”

Lunaris:
“…no… wrong time…”

---

## The Descent of Pride

> Read:

The tear does not close.

It widens.

Something steps through.

Not thrown.

Not forced.

Invited—

By itself.

---

## The Pride Entity

- Humanoid  
- Perfect posture  
- No wasted movement  

---

### Presence

- Reality seems to align around him  
- Sound dulls  
- Space feels… inferior  

---

### Dialogue

“A contest of strength?”

“How quaint.”

He looks at everyone.

Then—

Smiles slightly.

“I will take all of you.”

---

## Mammeth’s Reaction

This time—

Anger.

Real.

“…you dare.”

His voice sharpens.

“An inferior of Pride enters *my domain*?”

---

## Alethia Steps Forward

Calm.

But firm.

“This is no longer your domain alone.”

---

## Situation

- Mammeth has **no champions left**
- Pride Entity refuses structure
- Fradonic & Lunaris are weakened

---

## Vanka’s Intervention

> Read:

Before anything escalates—

Vanka steps forward.

No hesitation.

No fear.

“Enough.”

---

## Vaelithra Awakening

> Read:

The entire hotel hums.

Not violently.

Not chaotically.

Intentionally.

Time bends.

You feel something—

Looking at you.

Recognizing you.

### Narrative

Amused.

“Better.”

“Now… try.”
