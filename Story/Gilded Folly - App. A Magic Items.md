# Appendix A: Magic Items

*Gilded Folly* contains five magic items: four consumable "Room Service" gifts left by **Vanka Yuriv** ahead of each Trial, each countering that Trial's core mechanic, and one keepsake — **the Gilded Chronos-Bell** — given to every party member at the close of *Part V: Scion of Pride*.

## Item Descriptions

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=loot,collapse=--> The Spendthrift's Coin
> *Consumable, given before Trial I (Part I: The Gilded Cage)*
>
> A heavy gold coin that feels unnervingly warm.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> Use a bonus action to throw this coin at a target. It explodes into a shower of sparks. This automatically triggers **Cracks in the Hoard** on **Vauryx, Hoarder of Still Gold**, breaking his Aurum Stasis without the player needing to spend a resource of their own.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=loot,collapse=--> The Mirror-Polished Serving Tray
> *Consumable, given before Trial II (Part II: The First Trials)*
>
> A silver tray polished to a terrifying clarity. You see things in it that aren't there — or perhaps things that shouldn't be.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> Use an action to reflect light, or reality, at one target. Against **Feltheris, the Golden Warden**, this forces him to move 5 feet, breaking his Perfect Alignment. Against **Citrina, the Lustrous Siren**, she becomes fascinated by her own reflection, counting as ignored for 1 round and triggering her Gaze of Value penalties.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=loot,collapse=--> The Bellhop's Service Bell
> *Consumable, given before Trial III (Part III: The Weight of Ownership)*
>
> A simple silver bell. When it rings, the sound doesn't echo — it cuts through other sounds.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> Use a reaction when an enemy creates a zone or moves. Against **Auric Vex, Arbiter of Dominion**, this instantly collapses one Dominion Zone, or lets a player move 10 feet out of a zone without provoking opportunity attacks.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=loot,collapse=--> The Aromatic "Silence" Candle
> *Consumable, given before Trial IV (Part III: The Weight of Ownership)*
>
> The wax is grey and smells of nothing at all. It feels like a void in your palm.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> Use a bonus action to light the candle; it burns for 1 round. Against **Aurelion, the Keeper of Excess**, this resets all of his Accumulation stacks to 0. Against **Seraphel, the Bearer of Burden**, this breaks her Shared Burden link for 1 round.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=loot,collapse=--> The Gilded Chronos-Bell
> *Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Champion of Vaelithra)*
>
> A fragment of the Gilded Folly Jail Hotel, manifested as a small silver service bell. It makes no sound when shaken — only a wave of "stillness" that briefly freezes a moment in time. Vanka Yuriv gives one to each party member at the close of *Part V: Scion of Pride*, as the hotel dissolves around them.
>
> ___
>
> ### Phase-Lock Divinity
> Once per long rest, as a reaction when you or a creature you can see within 60 feet takes damage, you may ring this bell. That creature enters **Temporal Stasis** until the start of its next turn: its hit points cannot change in either direction, and it is immune to all damage and new conditions for the duration.
>
> ___
>
> ### Absolute Standard (Passive)
> While holding this bell, your attacks carry a minimum standard of force. If you deal less than 10 damage on a hit, the damage is instead treated as 10.
